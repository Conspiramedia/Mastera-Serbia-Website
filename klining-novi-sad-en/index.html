What we clean

  • Standard cleaning — regular upkeep: dust, floors, bathroom, kitchen. Works well as a weekly service, subscription available
  • Deep cleaning — the whole flat top to bottom: furniture inside and out, appliances, tiles, glass, hard-to-reach spots
  • Post-renovation cleaning — removing construction dust, paint, glue and cement marks. Windows, floors and surfaces washed back to clean
  • Window and balcony washing — glass, frames, sills and reveals washed on both sides. Streak-free, with professional products
  • Upholstery dry cleaning — sofas, armchairs, mattresses and carpets cleaned, with stains and odours removed. Dry the same day
  • After-party cleaning — getting the flat back in order fast: dishes, rubbish, stains, airing out. Can be booked for the morning after
  • Move-out cleaning — preparing a flat for new tenants or for handover to the landlord. Spotless, so your deposit comes back
  • Recurring cleaning subscription — a regular cleaner weekly or fortnightly at a reduced rate
