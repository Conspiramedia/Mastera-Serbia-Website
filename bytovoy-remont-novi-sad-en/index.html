What home jobs we do

  • Lock replacement and repair — mortise, rim, electronic. Opening jammed locks, replacing cylinders, fitting new locks on entrance and interior doors
  • Door repair and adjustment — fixing squeaks, misalignment and sagging. Replacing hinges, handles and closers, adjusting interior and entrance doors
