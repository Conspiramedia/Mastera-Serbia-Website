What we mount and install

  • TV wall mounting — fitting fixed, tilting and full-motion brackets. Concealed cable routing, fixing into concrete, brick and drywall
  • Shelves and shelving — level fixing of shelves of any weight, concealed fasteners, anchors selected for the wall type
