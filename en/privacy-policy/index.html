Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Pravi Majstor platforma (the “service”) collects through pravimajstor.rs and what we do with it. Processing follows the Serbian Personal Data Protection Act (“Sl. glasnik RS” No. 87/2018).

1. Data controller

Data is controlled by Pravi Majstor platforma, Belgrade, Serbia. Contact for data questions: kontakt@pravimajstor.rs, phone [TELEFON_381].

2. What we collect

Request data: name, phone number or Telegram, city and district, task description and, if you attach them, photos of the task.

Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited — via Google Analytics.

We do not collect special categories of data (health, religion, political views) and do not ask for them in the form.

3. Why we process it

Request data is used solely to pass your task to masters and to let a master contact you. Technical data is used for visit statistics and improving the site.

4. Who receives the data

The task description, city and district are visible to masters working in that area. Your contact details (phone or Telegram) are seen only by the master who accepts the request — until then the contact stays hidden.

We do not sell your data and do not pass it to third parties for marketing.

5. Storage and security

Contact details are stored encrypted. Access is limited to the service administrator and the master who accepted the request. Data is kept only as long as needed to complete the job and handle any complaints.

6. Your rights

You may request access to your data, its correction or deletion, and you may withdraw your consent to processing. Send requests to kontakt@pravimajstor.rs.

If you believe your rights have been violated, you may contact the Serbian Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection.

7. Cookies

The site uses cookies for Google Analytics and to remember your chosen language and city. You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings — the site will keep working.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date of the last change is shown at the bottom of this page.
