Frequently asked questions about prices

Is finding a handyman free?

Yes. The service is free for customers — we take no commission on the price of the work. You pay the handyman directly, at the agreed price.

Can the price change during the job?

No. The handyman inspects the job and states a fixed price before starting. If something hidden comes up, they agree it with you first.

Are materials included in the price?

No, the table shows the cost of labour. Materials are paid separately — the handyman can source them, or you can provide your own.
