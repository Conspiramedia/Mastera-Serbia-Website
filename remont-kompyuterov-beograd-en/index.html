What we do

  • Windows installation and reinstallation — clean system setup with drivers and essential software. We keep your files and configure the system for your needs
  • Virus and malware removal — cleaning the system of malicious software, ad banners and crypto miners. Protection set up so it does not happen again
  • Dust cleaning and thermal paste replacement — disassembly, cooling system cleaning, thermal paste replacement. Your computer stops overheating and running loud
  • Component replacement — SSD instead of HDD, extra RAM, power supply and other parts. An old laptop runs fast again

  • Laptop repair — keyboard, screen, hinge and power connector replacement. Free diagnostics when you proceed with the repair
  • Data recovery — rescuing files from damaged drives and flash memory, recovering accidentally deleted data
  • Software and office suite setup — MS Office, graphics and work software installation, email, cloud storage and backup configuration
