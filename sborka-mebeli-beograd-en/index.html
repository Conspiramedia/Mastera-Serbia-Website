What furniture we assemble

  • Cabinet furniture — wardrobes, sliding wardrobes, chests, cabinets, shelving. Assembly with or without instructions, facade fitting, door and hinge adjustment
  • Kitchen units — fitting lower and upper modules, wall-mounting cabinets, countertop installation, sink and hob cut-outs
  • Bedroom furniture — beds, beds with lifting mechanisms, bases, bedside tables. Reinforcing joints, eliminating squeaks
  • IKEA and other-store furniture — assembled by instructions and part numbers, with missing fasteners brought along and completeness checked
  • Disassembly and assembly for relocation — careful disassembly, labelling of parts and fasteners, reassembly at the new place with nothing lost
  • Office furniture — desks, shelving, document cabinets, conference furniture, chairs
  • Furniture repair and restoration — replacing hinges, runners, handles, legs. Eliminating play, misalignment and squeaks
  • Mounting and installation — shelves, mirrors, wall-mounted cabinets with secure fixing suited to the wall type
